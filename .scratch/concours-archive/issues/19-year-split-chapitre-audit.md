# 19: Tag year-split topics with the chapitre each exercise really uses

**What to build:** Some topics are taught in both years, so the program has one
chapitre for each year. For every exercise tagged with one of these chapitres,
read its pages and keep the chapitre it really uses. A student filtering on a
2ème-année chapitre should find its exercises there, not under the first-year
chapitre.

**Blocked by:** none

**Status:** done

### Boundaries (from myprepa.tn's chapter contents)

- **Séries numériques.** 1ère année: convergence, positive terms,
  comparison, Riemann series, absolute convergence, decimal expansion, and
  the series–integral comparison *for a monotone f*, used on partial sums and
  remainders. **Compléments (2ème année):** d'Alembert, the alternating-series
  criterion with the sign and bound of the remainder, summing comparison
  relations (O, o, ~) to get equivalents of sums or remainders, the Cauchy
  product of two numerical series, and Abel summation. myprepa lists Abel
  summation in this chapitre as "hors programme". That means the tool isn't
  required knowledge, not that the topic lies outside the program, so an
  exercise that walks the student through it still counts. A Cauchy product
  of two *power* series belongs to `series-entieres` instead. A
  series–integral comparison that needs an improper integral ("f integrable
  ⇔ Σf(n) converges") counts as 2ème année. An exercise also keeps
  `series-numeriques` when a separate block of its questions needs only the
  first-year tools.
- **Intégration.** On a segment (1ère année): Riemann sums, primitives, IPP
  and change of variable, Taylor. On an arbitrary interval: any improper
  integral or integrability question, including on ]0,1] or [0,1[.
  **Intégrales à paramètre:** continuity or differentiability of
  x ↦ ∫f(x,t)dt, including over a segment, and Fubini on segments.
- **Structures algébriques.** 1ère année: laws, groups and subgroups, rings,
  fields, the group of units. 2ème année: morphisms, image and kernel,
  subgroup generated by a part, monogenic and cyclic groups, order of an
  element, Z/nZ, ideals, arithmetic in K[X], algebras. An exercise that only
  checks that some set is a subgroup is 1ère année.
- **Préhilbertien.** 1ère année: scalar product, Cauchy–Schwarz,
  orthogonality, Schmidt, orthonormal bases, projection onto and distance to
  a finite-dimensional subspace, hyperplanes, isometries, reflections,
  Oₙ(R) and SOₙ(R), the plane case. 2ème année: symmetric endomorphisms and
  the spectral theorem, reduction of isometries (dimension n and 3), Bessel's
  inequality, total orthonormal sequences, sequences of orthogonal
  polynomials. The 2ème-année chapitre starts with "rappels" of the first,
  so Y1 notions used as background don't earn a Y1 tag. An exercise gets
  `prehilbertiens-y1` next to `-y2` only when a separate block is about Y1
  topics themselves (SO₂, reflections, a Gram-matrix part).

### Wrong tags or labels found on the way

These exercises weren't about the year question, but their tags described
another part of the paper:

- `cnc-maths1-2024-e` was tagged as series. It is ∫₀^∞ xⁿe^{−3x}dx.

  original scope.

  had labels and primaries naming probability or reduction, but their

  `variables-aleatoires-discretes`, which appears nowhere in their pages.
