# Concours Maths (MP) — Where to Legitimately Download Past Subjects

Research date: 2026-09-19. Scope: past **MP (Mathématiques–Physique)** Maths written subjects
(*sujets / épreuves écrites*) for **France, Tunisia, Morocco**. Priority given to **official /
first-party concours sources**. Aggregators are listed as clearly-labelled secondary options.

> **Note:** This file only *locates and cites* sources. No paper content was downloaded or
> reproduced. Direct-PDF URLs were read from each site's own "annales / sujets" page and may
> rotate as sites re-upload files each session — always re-check against the official annales page.

---

## 1. Summary — best entry point per country

- **France:** Every major banque d'épreuves publishes its own annales for free. Best official
  entry points: **Centrale-Supélec** (`concours-centrale-supelec.fr/sujets-rapports`, per-year
  PDFs 2017→2026), **Mines-Ponts** (`concoursminesponts.fr/annales/`, per-year ZIPs 2014→2026,
  under an explicit CC BY-NC-ND licence), and **CCINP** (`concours-commun-inp.fr` → Annales MP,
  per-year PDFs). These three cover almost the whole MP written-maths landscape.
- **Tunisia:** *(in progress — see section 3)*
- **Morocco:** *(in progress — see section 4)*

## 2. France (official banques d'épreuves)

### 2a. Concours Centrale-Supélec — OFFICIAL
- **Annales page:** https://www.concours-centrale-supelec.fr/sujets-rapports
  (tab **MP** → buttons *Mathématiques 1* / *Mathématiques 2* expand to per-year PDF links).
- **Direct MP Maths 1 subject PDFs** (base `https://www.concours-centrale-supelec.fr`):
  - 2026: `/sites/default/files/documents/M098.pdf`
  - 2025: `/sites/default/files/documents/M062.pdf`
  - 2024: `/sites/default/files/2025-01/M055.pdf`
  - 2023: `/sites/default/files/2025-01/M048.pdf`
  - 2022: `/sites/default/files/documents/M035.pdf`
  - 2021: `/sites/default/files/documents/M042.pdf`
  - 2020: `/sites/default/files/documents/M032.pdf`
  - 2019: `/sites/default/files/documents/M005_0.pdf`
- **Direct MP Maths 2 subject PDFs:**
  - 2026: `/sites/default/files/documents/M104.pdf`
  - 2025: `/sites/default/files/documents/M065.pdf`
  - 2024: `/sites/default/files/2025-01/M064.pdf`
  - 2023: `/sites/default/files/documents/M049.pdf`
  - 2022: `/sites/default/files/documents/M041.pdf`
  - 2021: `/sites/default/files/documents/M033.pdf`
  - 2020: `/sites/default/files/documents/M022.pdf`
  - 2019: `/sites/default/files/documents/M010.pdf`
- **Contains:** official written subjects (sujets), plus separate *Rapports du Jury*. Sujets
  only, no official corrigés.
- **Reliability:** First-party, authoritative. This is the exam authority's own site.
- **Usage/copyright:** The site did not display an explicit reuse licence on the annales page.
  Subjects are the concours' intellectual property; treat as "all rights reserved" unless the
  site's mentions légales say otherwise. Safe for personal/prépa use; **confirm before public
  redistribution/bundling.**

### 2b. Concours Commun Mines-Ponts (CCMP) — OFFICIAL  ← clearest reuse terms
- **Annales page:** https://www.concoursminesponts.fr/annales/
- **Direct 2026 MP Maths PDFs:**
  - Maths 1 (2026): https://concoursminesponts.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Maths-MP-1.pdf
  - Maths 2 (2026): https://concoursminesponts.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Maths-MP-2.pdf
- **Per-year full ZIP archives** ("Années antérieures", all filières bundled):
  - 2026: https://concoursminesponts.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/SUJETS-CCMP-2026.zip
  - 2025: https://concoursminesponts.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/SUJETS-2025.zip
  - 2024: https://wordpress.concoursminesponts.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2024.zip
  - 2023: https://wordpress.concoursminesponts.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2023.zip
  - 2022: https://wordpress.concoursminesponts.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2022.zip
  - 2021: https://wordpress.concoursminesponts.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2021.zip
  - 2020: https://wordpress.concoursminesponts.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2020.zip
  - (…continues yearly back to 2014)
- **Contains:** all written subjects per year/filière (MP incl. Maths 1 & 2), **no corrigés.**
- **Reliability:** First-party, authoritative.
- **Usage/copyright (stated on the page, verbatim gist):** Subjects are the property of GIP CCMP
  and published under **Creative Commons Attribution – Pas d'Utilisation Commerciale – Pas de
  Modification 4.0 France (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 FR)**. You may freely use/distribute a subject if you
  (1) mention it is a CCMP-owned subject and include a link to the licence, (2) make **no
  commercial use**, and (3) **do not modify** the subjects. Any other use needs prior CCMP
  authorisation. → **This is the most redistribution-friendly official French source, provided the
  destination site is non-commercial and papers are unmodified with attribution.**

### 2c. CCINP (Concours Commun INP, ex-CCP) — OFFICIAL
- **Annales hub:** https://www.concours-commun-inp.fr/fr/epreuves/annales.html → **Annales MP**:
  https://www.concours-commun-inp.fr/fr/epreuves/annales/annales-mp.html
- **Direct MP Maths subject PDFs** (base `https://www.concours-commun-inp.fr`, `?download=true`):
  - 2026 Maths 1: `/_resource/annales%20%C3%A9crits/2026/MP/2026_MP1M1.pdf`
  - 2026 Maths 2: `/_resource/annales%20%C3%A9crits/2026/MP/2026_MP3M2.pdf`
  - 2025 Maths 1: `/_resource/annales%20%C3%A9crits/2025/MP/Maths1_2025.pdf`
  - 2025 Maths 2: `/_resource/annales%20%C3%A9crits/2025/MP/Maths%202_2025.pdf`
  - 2024 Maths 1: `/_resource/annales%20%C3%A9crits/2024/MP/2024_MP1M1.pdf`
  - 2024 Maths 2: `/_resource/annales%20%C3%A9crits/2024/MP/2024_MP3M2.pdf`
- **Contains:** written subjects (sujets) + oral-maths *rapports*. Sujets, no corrigés.
- **Reliability:** First-party, authoritative.
- **Usage/copyright:** No explicit reuse licence shown on the annales page (site has Mentions
  Légales + RGPD pages). Treat as concours IP; confirm before public bundling.

### 2d. Concours X-ENS (Polytechnique / ENS) — official, less centralised
- No single clean "annales" portal equivalent to the above. X-ENS maths subjects are most reliably
  found via the aggregators in §2e (UPS, doc-solus) or the ENS/École polytechnique concours pages.
  *(Not fully verified this session — see Open questions.)*

### 2e. Reputable French aggregators — SECONDARY (not first-party)
- **UPS – Union des Professeurs de Spéciales** (`ups.prepas.org`): official-adjacent; hosts jury
  reports and cross-references. Reliable, teacher-run.
- **doc-solus.fr** (Doc Solus / "Prepas.org" annales): large searchable annales database across
  all banques including X-ENS; reliable but a commercial third party — use as a locator, prefer the
  official PDF when available.
- Reliability: good for coverage/finding older or X-ENS papers, but always prefer the official
  banque's own PDF for the canonical version and clear licensing.

## 3. Tunisia — Concours National d'entrée aux cycles de formation d'ingénieurs (MP)

### 3a. Concours-ingénieurs (DGET / MESRS) — OFFICIAL  ← best Tunisian source
- **Official platform:** https://concours-ingenieurs.rnu.tn/ (run for the Ministère de
  l'Enseignement Supérieur et de la Recherche Scientifique / DGET). This is the national concours'
  own site.
- **Annales page:** `EPREUVES` tab → https://concours-ingenieurs.rnu.tn/epreuves ("Liste des
  sujets des années antérieures", year toggles **2023 / 2024 / 2025 / 2026**). Selecting a year
  loads that year's per-subject PDFs, e.g. `?annee=2025`.
- **Direct MP Maths subject PDF (2025 verified)** (base `https://concours-ingenieurs.rnu.tn`):
  - 2025 MP Maths 1: `/uploads/sujets/2025/Maths%201-%20MP-2025.pdf`
  - Other 2025 MP subjects live in the same folder, e.g. `/uploads/sujets/2025/Physique-MP-T-2025.pdf`,
    `/uploads/sujets/2025/ChimieInorganique-MP-2025.pdf`, `/uploads/sujets/2025/Informatique-MP-PC-T-2025.pdf`.
  - Other years: swap the year in the path, e.g. `/uploads/sujets/2024/…` and `/uploads/sujets/2026/…`
    (browse via `?annee=2024` / `?annee=2026` to read exact filenames — naming is not perfectly
    uniform across years).
- **Contains:** official written subjects (sujets) by filière/subject. MP maths appears as a single
  "Maths 1 – MP" file per recent year (verify whether a Maths 2 exists for the year you need).
- **Reliability:** First-party, authoritative (the national concours platform itself).
- **Usage/copyright:** No reuse licence or terms were displayed on the épreuves page (Tunisian
  government/education platform). Treat as official exam IP; confirm before public redistribution.

### 3b. myprepa.tn — SECONDARY (Tunisian prépa aggregator/index)
- **Site:** https://www.myprepa.tn/ — advertises a "Liste des sujets de concours 2000–2025" and
  "Sujets et corrigés de concours en lecture", filière MP · PC · T · BG, cross-referencing concours
  sessions ~2018–2025.
- **Access:** Requires a **free account** (`/auth/signup`) to open the sujets index/reader; a paid
  "CPEI" tier bundles programmes + sujets + analyses du jury. Reading/annotation is in-browser.
- **Reliability:** Reputable, well-organised Tunisian prépa platform; good as a cross-reference and
  for older sessions (2000–2017) not on the official site, but it is a third party behind a login —
  prefer the official `concours-ingenieurs.rnu.tn` PDFs for the canonical files.
- **Usage/copyright:** Content is account-gated and hosted by a private platform; not a
  redistribution-friendly source. Do not re-bundle from here.

### 3c. Other Tunisian institutional mirrors (SECONDARY, official-adjacent)
- Prépa institutes publish "Sujets et corrections" pages, e.g. **IPEIEM** (`ipeiem.rnu.tn`),
  **IPEIM** (`ipeim.rnu.tn`). These `.rnu.tn` (university) sites mirror national concours subjects
  and are reliable, but coverage/format varies by institute. Useful as a fallback locator.

## 4. Morocco — CNC (Concours National Commun), filière MP

> **Finding:** Morocco's CNC is piloted by a different engineering school each year and does **not**
> maintain a single stable public "annales" portal the way the French banques do. In practice the
> subjects are released by the piloting school and mirrored by Moroccan prépa sites. The most
> complete, well-structured index is a reputable Moroccan teacher/prépa site (below). Treat all of
> these as **secondary/aggregator** sources — no first-party CNC archive was located this session.

### 4a. mathencpge.ma — SECONDARY (best-structured Moroccan index)  ← best Morocco entry point
- **MP annales page:** https://mathencpge.ma/annales/mp.html — clean table of **CNC MP Maths 1 &
  Maths 2 sujets + corrigés, 2011 → 2026**, one row per year.
- **How it hosts files:** the page links out to two hosts:
  - **Recent years (~2022–2026)** → hosted on **iamateacher.org** (site of *Prof ELAMIRI Ahmed*,
    agrégé de maths, prof de spé MP — a Moroccan teacher source). URLs are opaque hashes, e.g.
    - 2026 MP Maths 1: `https://www.iamateacher.org/_files/ugd/99b3c9_649b7ca65f3f4a7aabd9f28152609b82.pdf`
    - 2026 MP Maths 2: `https://www.iamateacher.org/_files/ugd/99b3c9_a6e74678df5a41ac8f34265cff8ffec5.pdf`
    - 2025 MP Maths 1: `https://www.iamateacher.org/_files/ugd/99b3c9_86ffc978d86d47ce9f8c1db228b409aa.pdf`
    - 2025 MP Maths 2: `https://www.iamateacher.org/_files/ugd/99b3c9_a867651a2fa24451893993993195c1e4.pdf`
    - (2024/2023 similar — open the mathencpge table to read each year's exact hashed link.)
  - **Older years (2011–2021)** → hosted on **groupe-reussite.fr** with clean, human-readable
    filenames, e.g.:
    - 2021 MP Maths 1: https://groupe-reussite.fr/ressources/wp-content/uploads/Annales/concours-maroc/cnc/cnc-maths1-mp-2021.pdf
    - 2021 MP Maths 2: https://groupe-reussite.fr/ressources/wp-content/uploads/Annales/concours-maroc/cnc/cnc-maths2-mp-2021.pdf
    - 2020 MP Maths 1: https://groupe-reussite.fr/ressources/wp-content/uploads/Annales/concours-maroc/cnc/cnc-maths1-mp-2020.pdf
    - 2019 MP Maths 1/2: `.../cnc/cnc-maths1-mp-2019.pdf`, `.../cnc/cnc-maths2-mp-2019.pdf`
    - Pattern back to 2011: `.../cnc/cnc-maths{1,2}-mp-{year}.pdf` (+ `-corrige.pdf` for solutions).
- **Contains:** CNC MP Maths 1 & Maths 2 sujets (and many corrigés).
- **Reliability:** mathencpge.ma is a well-maintained Moroccan CPGE site and the best single index;
  the actual PDFs live on a teacher site (iamateacher.org) and a French commercial tutoring site
  (groupe-reussite.fr). Good coverage, but none is the CNC exam authority itself.
- **Usage/copyright:** None of these state a reuse licence. groupe-reussite.fr is a commercial
  company hosting the papers as free resources; iamateacher.org is a personal teaching site. CNC
  subjects are the exam's IP. **Do not assume redistribution rights — confirm before bundling.**
- **Known bad file — CNC 2017 MP Maths 2 (ticket 18, 2026-09-22):** three mirrors serve the
  **Maths I** subject under a Maths 2 name: groupe-reussite's `cnc-maths2-mp-2017.pdf` (1,072,104
  bytes), rtc.ma's `CNC 2017 Math-2 MP.pdf` and alloschool's `cnc-mp-2017-maths-2-epreuve.pdf` (both
  720,196 bytes). The real Maths II (one problem: matrix norms, matrix exponential, differential
  systems) is iamateacher.org's "2017-2-énoncé",
  `https://www.iamateacher.org/_files/ugd/99b3c9_b5f540b690084c73a15b26ae975b6649.pdf` (304,174
  bytes, footer "Épreuve de Mathématiques II"). Check page content, not file names.

### 4b. Other Moroccan aggregators (SECONDARY)
- **alloschool.com**, **masup.ma**, **rtc.ma** (Road To CNC), **cpge-paradise.com** — all host CNC
  MP maths annales with varying completeness. Useful as cross-checks/mirrors; same copyright caveat.

## 7. Open questions

1. **X-ENS (Polytechnique/ENS):** no single official annales portal was verified this session.
   Next step: locate X-ENS MP maths sujets via doc-solus.fr or the ENS/Polytechnique concours pages,
   and confirm whether an official first-party archive exists.
   - **Follow-up (2026-09-20):** re-checked while extending the archive. **doc-solus.fr** is a
     commercial *in-browser reader* (`view.corrige.html?q=MP_MATHS_<BRAND>_<n>_<year>`), not a source
     of downloadable PDFs, and it points to **H&K** (`h-k.fr/adc`), a paid annales publisher. Neither
     is redistribution-appropriate for this non-commercial archive. Quick probes of
     `concours-polytechnique.fr`, `scei-concours.fr/annales.php`, `ups.prepas.org` did not surface a
     free first-party X-ENS *sujet* archive (UPS hosts jury *rapports*, not sujets). WebSearch/WebFetch
     still fail in this environment. **Decision: X-ENS left out** until a first-party or explicitly
     reuse-licensed source is found — do not bundle from doc-solus/H&K.
2. **Morocco CNC official archive:** no first-party CNC portal with a public annales archive was
   found — the CNC is piloted by a rotating school each year. Worth confirming whether the current
   piloting school or a ministry portal (a `*.ac.ma` / `*.gov.ma` domain) publishes official PDFs,
   to replace the aggregator links with canonical versions.
3. **Tunisia MP Maths 2:** the official site showed a single "Maths 1 – MP" file for 2025; confirm
   whether the Tunisian MP concours has a second maths épreuve and, if so, locate its PDF.
4. **Licensing for bundling:** only Mines-Ponts states explicit reuse terms (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 FR).
   Before publicly bundling Centrale, CCINP, Tunisian, or Moroccan papers, the owner should check
   each site's mentions légales / obtain permission, since none of them grant an explicit licence.
